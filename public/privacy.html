Walk it 3D privacy

Applies to the Walk it 3D app and website. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

Walk it 3D collects no data. Your walks and your revealed map stay on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

Your position is used only during a walk you start, to open the map where you walk. In the iPhone app it keeps recording while the screen is locked, until you end the walk; iOS shows its location sign meanwhile. Positions are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Map data

The app downloads public map pieces (OpenStreetMap data through OpenFreeMap, and terrain heights from the public terrain tiles on Amazon Web Services). These requests ask for a map area, like any map app; they never include your walk, and the app adds no identifier to them. These services may see your IP address, as any website does.

When you search for a place to save for offline use, the text you type is sent to the OpenStreetMap search service (Nominatim).

Compass

The compass reads your device's direction sensors on the device. Nothing is stored or sent.

Deleting

Settings › Delete my walks and map removes your walks and map. Settings › Delete saved map data removes downloaded map pieces. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
